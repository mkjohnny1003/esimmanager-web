Android 版:你的健康資料如何被處理

本政策適用於「我的血壓」Android App(套件名稱 com.mkjohnny.bpcare,以下稱本 App)。iPhone 與 Apple Watch 版請見這一頁。

English version ↓

1. 健康資料只存在你的手機

你輸入的血壓、心率、用藥、服藥紀錄、回診、慢性病連續處方箋、攜帶物品清單與藥物照片,都只保存在你手機上本 App 的私有儲存空間。開發者不經營任何伺服器,也沒有 App 帳號,因此無法看到、取得或出售你的健康資料。本 App 關閉了 Android 的自動備份,這些資料不會被上傳到 Google 雲端備份。

2. 本 App 不會讀取的資料

本 App 不讀取或寫入 Health Connect,也不讀取你的通訊錄、簡訊、通話紀錄或 GPS 定位,不會要求這些權限。

3. 相機與照片

你為藥丸或藥袋拍的照片只保存在本 App 內。「拍血壓計自動帶入數字」只會出現在支援裝置端 AI(Gemini Nano)的手機上:照片由 Google ML Kit 在你的手機上辨識數字,不會上傳到任何伺服器,也不會存到相簿。照片只暫存在本 App 的私有暫存區,下次拍照時會被取代,你也可以在 Android 設定中清除本 App 的快取。為了維護與改善服務,Google ML Kit 可能會把功能使用情形與診斷資料傳給 Google,但不包含你的照片與健康資料。

4. 通知與提醒

量血壓、吃藥、回診與領藥提醒都是手機本機通知。「鬧鐘與提醒」權限只用來讓提醒準時響起。這個版本沒有任何自動傳訊息給家人的功能。

5. 廣告(Google AdMob)

免費版會顯示 Google AdMob 提供的非個人化廣告。本 App 不會把任何健康資料提供給廣告服務。為了顯示廣告、計算成效與防止詐欺,Google 可能會收集裝置與網路資訊,例如 IP 位址(可推估大致位置)、Android 廣告 ID 與應用程式集 ID、裝置型號與系統版本、廣告互動紀錄及診斷資料。你可以在 Android 設定的「隱私權 › 廣告」重設或刪除廣告 ID;所在地區需要時,本 App 會詢問你的廣告同意,之後可在 App 的「設定 › 廣告 › 廣告隱私選項」修改。詳情請見 Google 隱私權政策與 Google 如何使用合作夥伴網站或應用程式的資訊。購買「永久移除廣告」後,本 App 不再載入任何廣告。

6. 購買

「永久移除廣告」由 Google Play 處理付款,開發者不會取得你的付款資訊。本 App 只會向 Google Play 確認你是否已購買。

7. 你主動分享的內容

回診 PDF 報告、CSV 匯出檔,以及「備份與還原」的備份檔,都只會在你按下分享或匯出時產生,並由你選擇存放位置或傳送對象(例如雲端硬碟、Email、醫師或家人)。備份檔含有你的健康資料,請妥善保管。

8. 資料保存與刪除

解除安裝本 App,或在 Android 設定中清除本 App 的儲存空間,就會刪除 App 內的所有資料。你自行匯出的備份檔,請在你存放的位置刪除。Google 為廣告收集的資料,依 Google 隱私權政策保存與刪除。

9. 兒童

本 App 以成年人為對象,並非為兒童設計。

10. 醫療聲明

本 App 僅供個人健康紀錄與提醒使用,不是醫療器材。分級參考 2022 台灣高血壓治療指引,不能取代專業醫療診斷。個人的控制目標請以醫師建議為準。

11. 政策變更與聯絡方式

本政策如有更新,會公布在本頁並更新日期。若有任何隱私問題,請來信 mkjohnny@gmail.com。

Privacy Policy (Android)

This policy applies to the 我的血壓 (My Blood Pressure) Android app, package name com.mkjohnny.bpcare (the "App"). For the iPhone and Apple Watch app, see this page. The App's interface is in Traditional Chinese; English names of settings are given in parentheses.

1. Health data stays on your phone

The blood pressure, heart rate, medications, dose records, appointments, chronic prescription refills, checklists and medication photos you enter are stored only in the App's private storage on your phone. The developer does not operate any server and the App has no accounts, so the developer cannot see, obtain or sell your health data. The App turns off Android Auto Backup, so this data is not uploaded to Google cloud backup.

2. Data the App does not access

The App does not read or write Health Connect and does not access your contacts, text messages, call log or GPS location. It does not request these permissions.

3. Camera and photos

Photos you take of your pills or medication bags are stored only inside the App. 拍血壓計 (Scan BP monitor) is offered only on phones that support on-device AI (Gemini Nano): Google ML Kit reads the numbers on your phone, and the photo is never uploaded to any server or saved to your photo library. The photo is kept only in the App's private cache until your next scan replaces it, and you can clear the App's cache in Android settings. To maintain and improve the service, Google ML Kit may send feature usage and diagnostic data to Google; this never includes your photos or health data.

4. Notifications and reminders

Reminders to measure blood pressure, take medication, attend appointments and pick up refills are local notifications on your phone. The Alarms & reminders permission is used only so that reminders ring on time. This version has no feature that sends messages to family members automatically.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows non-personalized ads provided by Google AdMob. The App never provides any health data to advertising services. To serve ads, measure performance and prevent fraud, Google may collect device and network information such as IP address (which can indicate approximate location), the Android advertising ID and app set ID, device model and OS version, ad interactions and diagnostic data. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads. Where required in your region, the App asks for your ad consent, and you can change it later in the App under 設定 › 廣告 › 廣告隱私選項 (Settings › Ads › Ad privacy options). See the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. After you purchase 永久移除廣告 (Remove Ads Permanently), the App no longer loads any ads.

6. Purchases

Payment for Remove Ads Permanently is handled by Google Play. The developer does not receive your payment information. The App only checks with Google Play whether you have made the purchase.

7. Content you choose to share

PDF visit reports, CSV exports and backup files from 備份與還原 (Backup & Restore) are created only when you tap share or export, and you choose where they are saved or who receives them (for example Google Drive, email, your doctor or family). Backup files contain your health data; please keep them safe.

8. Data retention and deletion

Uninstalling the App, or clearing the App's storage in Android settings, deletes all data in the App. Backup files you exported yourself should be deleted from wherever you stored them. Data Google collects for advertising is kept and deleted under the Google Privacy Policy.

9. Children

The App is intended for adults and is not designed for children.

10. Medical disclaimer

The App is for personal health records and reminders only and is not a medical device. Its blood pressure categories follow the 2022 Taiwan Hypertension Guidelines. It does not replace professional medical diagnosis; please follow your doctor's advice for your personal targets.

11. Changes and contact

Any updates to this policy will be posted on this page with a new date. For privacy questions, email mkjohnny@gmail.com.
