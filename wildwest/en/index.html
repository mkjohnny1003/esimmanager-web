How to play

Four spots, one revolver, a whole saloon

The sheriff has four spots on the floor and fires straight up the whole column. Read the bartender's rhythm, and keep an eye on the customers.

🍾 Shoot

Smash plates, bottles and glasses. One, two or three items in one shot score 10, 50 or 150.

🍎 Dodge

His ashtrays land on the two left spots, her apples on the two right spots. When someone stands up, move.

🧨 Dynamite & whiskey

From the 2nd pattern, shooting dynamite is a miss. In the 3rd, walk to the far right and press RIGHT once more to pour.

🤠 Showdown

The desperado fakes. The overturned table stops only 3 bullets; hit him 3 times to win.

Levels & scoring

10 levels × 3 patterns, faster and faster

Higher levels bring faster beats and more items at once. After level 10 it loops back to level 6. Three misses in one pattern is GAME OVER.

Leaderboard

Game Center high scores

Signed in to Game Center, your score is posted at every GAME OVER. Tap RANK below the screen to see the global leaderboard. You can play without signing in.

Handheld feel

Segments, ghosting, square-wave sound

Characters don't glide — fixed segments light up in turn. Every sound is synthesized live from square waves and noise, with haptic feedback. Sound plays even with the silent switch on; tap ♪ on the body to mute.

Controls

Touch, keyboard or controller

Use the on-screen FIRE button and L/R rocker, a Bluetooth keyboard (← → to move, Space to fire), or an MFi, Xbox or PlayStation controller.

Price & privacy

Free, no ads, no in-app purchases

No ads, no tracking, no account and no in-app purchases. Your high score and settings stay on your device.
