Eat big. Regret slowly.

GUTSY is a pixel handheld-style action game about delayed growth. Food does not make you longer right away; it sits in your body, moves down the snake, and only counts after it digests.

Download on the App Store Open-source web version Support

Core Loop

Every bite is a delayed bet

Delayed digestion

Food becomes a lump in your body and only adds length after it travels and digests.

Stomach capacity

Overeat and you burst. Starve and you shrink. The best move is rarely the safest one.

Tactical items

Poison, skates, bombs, diamonds, and enzymes can save a run or push it out of control.

Ten worlds

Grass, deserts, brick hazards, clockwork, coral, singularities, and more across 100 stages.

Controls

Portrait and landscape

Use the on-screen D-pad and A/B buttons. Standard mode progresses stage by stage; Map Mode lets you practice specific worlds and sub-stages.

Leaderboard

Survive longer, dare harder

Scores combine maximum length, survival time, and high-risk bonus actions. Game Center is optional.

Privacy

Free, no ads, no IAP

GUTSY has no ads, no tracking, no account system, and no in-app purchases. Progress is stored locally on your device.

Web Prototype

Open-source first 3 worlds

The browser prototype is available on GitHub with the first 3 worlds. The full 10-world game is on iPhone.
