名片資料與文字辨識

本 App 免註冊、免登入。名片照片、辨識文字、姓名、聯絡方式、備註與群組儲存在裝置的 App 專屬空間。我們沒有接收這些名片資料的雲端伺服器。文字辨識使用隨 App 提供的 Google ML Kit 模型，在裝置端執行；輸入圖片與辨識結果不會因 OCR 而傳送到 Google 或外部 AI 服務。

SDK 技術資料

裝置端 OCR 不代表所有 SDK 都完全不連網。依 Google ML Kit 官方說明，SDK 會收集安裝識別碼、裝置與 App 資訊、功能使用事件、效能與錯誤診斷資料，用於診斷與使用分析，經 HTTPS 傳輸；不包含名片圖片與辨識文字。我們未另外加入 Firebase Analytics 或當機回報 SDK。詳見 ML Kit 資料揭露及 Google 隱私權政策。

使用者控制的分享與權限

相機權限用於拍攝名片。相簿透過系統照片選取器只讀取您選擇的圖片。名片不會自動加入手機通訊錄；只有您選擇匯出時才交由系統聯絡人介面處理。選擇電話、Email、網站、分享或地圖時，相關欄位會傳給您選擇的外部 App，其後的處理由該服務負責。

商務行事曆功能可用時，需您授予行事曆讀寫權限、選擇可寫入的行事曆並確認，才新增相識或拜訪事件與提醒。App 讀取可用行事曆與自己建立的事件識別資料，不讀取其他事件內容；寫入姓名、日期、場所與循環設定，不包含電話、Email、備註或 OCR 全文。若您選擇同步行事曆或聯絡人帳戶，系統可能同步到該帳戶服務。

廣告與購買

目前 Android 1.2 首輪測試版的正式廣告關閉。套件包含 AdMob、Unity Ads、Liftoff SDK，但 App 的廣告開關未啟用。未來啟用廣告前會更新相應資料揭露與隱私選項。

一般版本使用 Google Play Billing 查詢可購買商品與購買狀態。付款由 Google Play 處理，我們不取得信用卡資料。購買可用後，App 會在裝置保存經驗證的購買記錄與簽章，用於恢復永久解鎖及確認退款狀態。1.2 封測試用版（build 3 起）暫時免費開放商務地圖與行事曆，不啟動付款、不查詢購買，也不產生永久購買記錄；正式版將恢復一次付費解鎖。

保存、刪除與安全

一般名片保留到您刪除，或清除 App 儲存空間／解除安裝為止；App 未啟用 Android 自動雲端備份。系統相簿原圖、已匯出的聯絡人、分享檔案與行事曆事件需在對應 App 另行刪除。Google 的 SDK 技術資料依其政策保存，我們無法直接識別或刪除 Google 持有的 SDK 記錄，可向 Google 提出相關請求。

本測試版尚未提供私密名片加密鎖與完整備份，請使用示範或已獲同意的資料測試。我們不宣稱 App 提供端對端加密保管庫。請妥善保護裝置鎖定與系統帳戶。

聯絡與政策更新

本產品面向成年商務使用者，不以兒童為對象。隱私問題、資料請求或支援請聯絡 mkjohnny@gmail.com。您主動寄送的信箱、訊息與附件會用於回覆及處理問題；請勿寄送完整名片、付款資料或密碼。問題處理後可要求刪除支援郵件，法律要求保存者除外。功能或資料處理方式變更時，本頁會更新日期與內容。

Android Privacy Policy

Card Master: Card Scanner (人脈大師), by TU TSUNG YING. Updated September 13, 2026. Applies to the Android 1.2 test release.

Local cards and recognition

No app account is required. Card photos, recognized text, contact fields, notes and groups stay in app-specific storage on your device. We do not operate a server that receives your cards. Bundled Google ML Kit performs OCR on the device; OCR images and results are not sent to Google or an external AI service.

Technical SDK data

ML Kit may transmit installation identifiers, device and app information, feature usage events, performance metrics and error diagnostics to Google for diagnostics and usage analytics over HTTPS. This excludes card images and recognized text. We have not added a separate analytics or crash-reporting SDK. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure and privacy policy linked above.

Permissions and actions you choose

Camera access is used to photograph cards; the system photo picker supplies only selected images. Cards are not automatically added to your phone contacts. Export, phone, email, web, sharing and map actions send the relevant fields to the external app you choose. Its provider then handles those fields.

When business calendar features are available, calendar read/write access is requested to list writable calendars and identify events created by this app, not to read unrelated event contents. Only after your confirmation does the app write a name, date, meeting place and recurrence/reminder settings. It does not export phone numbers, email, notes or OCR text into events. Your selected contacts or calendar account may sync through its own provider.

Advertising and purchases

Production advertising is disabled in this initial Android 1.2 test. The bundle includes AdMob, Unity Ads and Liftoff SDKs; the app's advertising switch is off. Disclosures and privacy controls will be updated before advertising is enabled. The standard version uses Google Play Billing to query products and purchase status. Google handles payment details; we do not receive card numbers. Verified purchase records and signatures may be stored on-device for permanent unlock restoration and refund checks. The closed beta trial (build 3 onward) temporarily includes business maps and calendars at no charge. It does not start billing, query purchases or create a permanent purchase record. The production version will return to a one-time paid unlock.

Retention and deletion

Delete cards in the app, clear its storage or uninstall to remove local app data. Android automatic cloud backup is disabled. Photos in your gallery and previously exported contacts, files or calendar events must be deleted separately in their respective apps. Google retains SDK data under its policies; we cannot directly identify or erase Google's SDK records. You can contact Google for related requests.

The current test does not provide an encrypted private-card vault or complete backup. Use sample or permission-based test data and protect your device and accounts. This app targets adult business users, not children.

Contact

Contact mkjohnny@gmail.com for support or privacy requests. Information you voluntarily email is used to answer and resolve your request. Do not send full cards, payment details or passwords. You may request deletion of support messages after resolution, except where retention is legally required. This page will be updated when our features or data practices change.
