PRIVACY POLICY · September 22, 2026

How Your Card Data Is Handled

This privacy policy applies to the Card Master iPhone app and this website, covering English, Traditional Chinese and Japanese editions.

1. Business Cards and Recognition

Card photos, recognized text, names, companies, phone numbers, email addresses, postal addresses, tags, and notes are stored locally on your device. Card Master utilizes Apple's on-device recognition and never transmits card contents to external AI services, advertising networks, or our own servers. Core recognition, editing, and search operate completely offline without requiring an account.

2. Private Cards and Cryptographic Keys

Card records are encrypted with device keys. Private cards additionally require Face ID, Touch ID, or device passcode authentication. Device-specific keys do not transfer automatically across devices, and removing your passcode may invalidate private keys. The app cannot prevent screenshots, jailbreak exploits, or physical device compromises.

3. Backups and External Copies

You may export an encrypted backup file alongside a 256-bit Recovery Code. Our support team cannot recover lost codes. Exported plaintext VCF or CSV files and synchronized system contacts leave the protection of the app's private vault.

4. Advertising

The free version utilizes Google AdMob (which may serve ads via Liftoff Monetize and Unity Ads). These providers may process IP addresses, device identifiers, and diagnostic interaction logs. The app requests non-personalized ads, disables publisher first-party IDs, and never passes business card content to ad networks. Purchasing the lifetime ad removal permanently ceases ad loading.

5. In-App Purchases

The Apple App Store processes all payments via StoreKit. Card Master does not receive financial or credit card data. The lifetime ad removal is a one-time non-consumable purchase, not a subscription.

6. Device Permissions

Camera access is used for card capture; photo picker reads selected card photos; biometric authentication safeguards private cards; Contacts and Calendar permissions are requested when you choose to access the relevant feature.

7. Data Deletion and Support

You can delete cards within the app at any time. Deleting the app removes local databases. When contacting support, never include client business cards, confidential records, or recovery codes.

8. Contact

Privacy inquiries can be directed to mkjohnny@gmail.com.

Website analytics

This website uses Google Analytics 4 to measure page usage and App Store link clicks. Browser and network information may be sent to Google. Website analytics do not include the app’s business card contents.
