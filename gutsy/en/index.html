Eat big. Regret slowly.

Gaboon Viper (formerly GUTSY) is a pixel handheld-style action game about delayed growth. Food does not make you longer right away; it sits in your body, moves down the snake, and only counts after it digests.

Mechanics Deep Dive

Every bite is a delayed bet

Unlike classic snake games where eating instantly lengthens the body, growth in Gaboon Viper is a delayed, physical digestive process with real risks and weight.

🥚 Delayed Digestion

Swallowed eggs form lumps inside the body, moving backward with each step, and only add length once fully metabolized.

💥 Stomach Capacity (BURST)

Your GUT meter tracks load in Green (safe) → Yellow (caution) → Red (critical). Overeating beyond 100% causes an instant BURST game over!

⌛ Starvation & Atrophy

Eating resets the 6-second hunger timer. If you starve past 6s, your body shrinks 1px every 2 seconds until you wither away (TOO EMPTY).

🏆 Three Game Modes

Includes Standard Mode across 10 worlds (100 stages), Map Mode for stage practice, and the new 5-minute Time Attack rush.

Tactical Items

Master items to survive and triumph

Six randomized tactical pickups appear across the field, offering strategic relief and game-changing bonuses.

🧪 Laxative / Poison Emergency Cleanse

Instantly purges all undigested lumps and shrinks length by 3px to avoid bursting. Crucially, shrinking never penalizes your historical MAX score multiplier!

⛸️ Roller Skates Speed Surge

Grants an 18% movement speed boost and +50 points. Perfect for weaving through tight corridors and sharp turns at high velocity.

🩴 Wooden Clog Steady Brake

The opposite of skates: slows you down, and one clog cancels out one pair of skates. Resets when you clear a stage, +50 points. Hit the brakes when you're stuffed and need steadier turns!

💣 Cannonball Bomb Deadly Hazard

Randomly rolling lethal mines that detonate on contact. However, if struck while invulnerable (Diamond), you defuse it for an extra +100 point bounty!

💎 Diamond Invincibility

Rare tactical gemstone (7.5% spawn) granting +200 points and 8 seconds of rainbow invincibility, enabling you to smash through bombs and hazards safely.

⚗️ Enzyme Metabolism Overdrive

Awards +50 points and triples digestion speed for 5 seconds, rapidly turning stomach lumps into length and clearing gut space in a flash.

☠️ Seven Death Conditions Full Analysis

The game strictly monitors 7 causes of death: BURST (overeating), WALL (boundary crash), BITE (self collision), TOO EMPTY (starvation), BOMB (mine blast), HAZARD (obstacles), and RIVAL (touching the rival snake).

Controls

Portrait and landscape

Use the on-screen D-pad and A/B buttons. Standard mode progresses stage by stage; Map Mode lets you practice specific worlds, and Time Attack offers a 5-minute rush.

Scoring Formula

Survive longer, dare harder

Official Score Formula: Max Length (MAX) × 5 + Survival Time × 2 + Item / Stage Bonuses. Compete globally on Game Center (Leaderboard ID: gutsy_max_len)!

Privacy

Free, no ads, no IAP

Gaboon Viper has no ads, no tracking, no account system, and no in-app purchases. Progress is stored locally on your device.
