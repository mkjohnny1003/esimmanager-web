SUPPORT & USER GUIDE

User Guide & Security Support

Thank you for choosing Card Master. We operate strictly on-device with zero cloud retention. Here is your essential guide to smooth operation and data security:

1. Recognition & Proofreading

Card Master processes text directly using Apple's on-device neural framework. No card images or text leave your device. For best results, ensure good lighting and avoid glare. You can tap recognized text blocks in the editor to quickly assign them to name, phone, or company fields.

2. Face ID & Touch ID Private Vault

Ensure your iPhone has a device passcode configured before using private cards. Once locked, sensitive cards can only be viewed after biometric or passcode verification. The app automatically locks upon entering the background or after 60 seconds of idle time.

3. Device Migration & Encrypted Backups

Because we do not maintain a cloud database, when upgrading to a new iPhone, export an Encrypted Backup in Settings and save the unique 256-bit Recovery Code. Enter this code on your new iPhone to restore your cards in seconds. Without the Recovery Code, our support team cannot decrypt your data.

4. Lifetime Ad Removal & Restoring Purchases

Ad removal is a one-time non-consumable purchase ($0.99), not a recurring subscription. On a new device, tap "Restore Purchases" under Settings with your Apple ID to regain lifetime ad-free status and business schedule tools immediately.

5. Contact Support

If you encounter issues or have questions, contact us directly at mkjohnny@gmail.com.
For your privacy, do not email client business cards, confidential details, or backup recovery codes.
