Privacy Policy · October 13, 2026

How Wild Showdown handles your data

This policy covers the Wild Showdown iPhone app and this website.

1. Data on your device

Your high score, best level and sound setting are stored on your device. They are never sent to our servers (we don't run any). Deleting the app removes them permanently.

2. Game Center leaderboard

The app can optionally use Apple Game Center; you can play the full game without signing in. When you are signed in, your score is posted to the Game Center leaderboard at the end of each game, and your nickname and score are handled and displayed under Apple's rules. We don't keep a copy. See the Apple Privacy Policy.

3. Ads, tracking and purchases

The app and this website contain no ads or ad SDKs, do no cross-app or cross-site tracking, and include no analytics. The app has no in-app purchases and needs no account.

4. Permissions

The app does not ask for camera, photos, contacts, location, microphone or notification permission.

5. Children's privacy

We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

6. Website hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process connection and security logs under its own policy. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

7. Contact and changes

For privacy questions, email mkjohnny@gmail.com. If anything changes, we will update the date above and this page.
