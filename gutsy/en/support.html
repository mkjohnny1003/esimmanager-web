Frequently Asked Questions

My progress or best score disappeared

Progress is stored locally on your device. Deleting the app, restoring your iPhone, or moving to a new device may remove local records. Cloud sync is not currently available.

I cannot see the global leaderboard

Please make sure you are signed in to Game Center in iOS Settings and allow the app to connect. You can play the full game without Game Center; the leaderboard simply will not appear.

Controls

Use the D-pad to move. A/B trigger in-game actions, with SELECT, START, and mute controls below the screen. GUTSY supports portrait and landscape play. The in-game HOW TO PLAY screen has the full rules.

Open-source web version

The web prototype is available on GitHub and includes the first 3 worlds for browser play. The iPhone version includes all 10 worlds, landscape mode, and Game Center leaderboards.

Report a problem or suggestion

Email mkjohnny@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, and steps to reproduce the issue. Do not send device passwords, Apple ID passwords, or payment information.
