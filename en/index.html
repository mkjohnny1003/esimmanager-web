Core Commitments

Choose your plan; manage to eSIMManager

No single supplier, no subscription thresholds, to help you organize dispersed travel eSIM information into a safe departure process.

Freedom to choose suppliers

Whether you get an eSIM from a travel platform, carrier, or other trusted outlet, you can organize the available activation information into an app. Offer prices, inventory, and service content will still be based on the vendor page you select.

Centralize multiple eSIMs

Keep the QR Code, LPA activation information, where applicable, expiration date, and memo in one place. Data is stored locally on your device; you can back up, share, or mark a trip as ended as needed.

Help set up with peace of mind-all for free

There is no in-app purchase or maximum number of cards, and there are installation guides, pre-departure confirmations, expiration countdowns, and device traffic estimates. Your iPhone's mobile data and roaming settings still need to be confirmed in the system.

Overview of real-world functions

Explore the main workflows and usage limitations through screens from the released app.

Pre-departure settings from the homepage

After creating a travel eSIM, the homepage provides a pre-departure portal for users to confirm important items in order.

  • Five steps: Installation, Cellular Data, Master Card Protection, Roaming and Connectivity.
  • Primary Card Protection: Remind to turn off the main card data roaming and mobile data switching.
  • Set Path: Use the navigation path in iPhone Settings to help newbies check.

Camera, Album, PDF and Manual 4-in-1 Import

Supports import of common LPA QR codes, images, PDFs, and full LPA strings; recognition depends on certificate format, document content, and image quality.

  • Camera Scanning: Aim for automatic parsing of paper or on-screen QR codes.
  • Album recognition: Read the QR code from the photo via the Apple Vision framework.
  • PDF Import: Directly select the PDF certificate document sent by the supplier.
  • Enter manually: Paste the full LPA activation code provided by the supplier.

Open the iOS eSIM installation process

When the information is complete and device support is enabled, you can try to open Apple's system eSIM installation process; it must still be confirmed by the user on the system screen, and the installation results are based on iOS and the vendor.

  • Installation flow guide: Click the button to display the iOS eSIM installation screen.
  • Confirm manually: The app can't silently install, enable, or change your iPhone's mobile settings.
  • Conditions of use: Requires iOS 17.5+, eSIM-enabled iPhone, active information, and internet connection.

Primary card protection to avoid misuse of roaming data

Make sure your primary card's “Data Roaming” and “Allow Mobile Data Switching” are turned off before you leave the country, reducing the risk of high roaming charges.

  • Master Card Data Roaming: Confirm deactivation in Settings > Mobile Services > Master Card.
  • Mobile data toggle: Confirm to turn off in Settings > Mobile Services > Mobile Data.
  • Will not change automatically: The app only provides onboarding and confirmation, and the setup is still done by the user on the iPhone.

Choose a language you're familiar with in the app

You can switch the language of the app interface directly during your trip, or choose to follow the language of the iPhone system.

  • Traditional Chinese/Simplified Chinese: Supports frequently used interfaces for Taiwanese and Chinese-speaking travelers.
  • English/Japanese/: Supports language selection in key travel markets.
  • Follow the system: Displays automatically according to your iPhone's app language settings.

Switching Phone Backup and Restore

You can export a JSON backup and import card records on a new device; when you set a password, the backup is encrypted with AES-GCM.

  • 🔐 Optional password encryption: After setting the password, it is encrypted with AES-256-GCM; backups without a password are not encrypted, please keep it properly.
  • 📁 Flexible storage: Can be sent in the File app, iCloud Drive, or AirDrop.
  • Sensitive information exclusion: The activation code is excluded by default; such backups restore the card history but cannot be used directly for system installation.

Technical Transparency and Functional Disclaimer

In order to protect the user's right to know and to align the system with how it really works, we would like to explain the technical rationale and limitations of this app:

"Traffic is device-side estimation" Technical Instructions

The mobile traffic statistics of this app are collected through the Darwin BSD underlying getifaddrs interface, reading local pdp_ip* The cumulative number of transceiver bytes in the mobile data channel. This isDevice-side network traffic estimation reference: Unable to identify which eSIM is actually being used, traffic from other SIMs, interface resets, data waiver offers, and billing cycles can all cause discrepancies. Estimates are updated when the app returns to the outlook, not the carrier's live bill; actual remaining balance is subject to the carrier's official system.

iOS System Installation Process and Permission Regulations

Due to Apple's iOS system security, third-party apps cannot silently install eSIM directly in the background, nor can they change mobile data lines for users. Clicking “Trigger Installation” will only attempt to open Apple's system eSIM installation process; the process and ability to complete the installation will depend on the iOS version, device, network, credentials, and vendor. The user still needs to manually confirm on the system screen.
