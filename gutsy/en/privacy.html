Privacy Policy · September 28, 2026

How GUTSY handles game data

This policy applies to the GUTSY iPhone app and the browser game on this website.

1. iPhone app data

Best scores, unlocked stages, sound, and display settings are stored on your device, not sent to our servers. Deleting the app removes these local records.

2. Game Center leaderboard

The iPhone app optionally uses Apple Game Center. You can play the full single-player game without signing in. If you sign in, Apple handles your nickname and scores under its rules. We do not separately store that Game Center data. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

3. Browser game and public leaderboard

Local browser progress and scores awaiting submission stay in your browser. After a run, your score is sent only if you choose "Submit score." We store your chosen player name, score, world and stage, difficulty, duration, completion status, and submission time in Cloudflare D1. The leaderboard publicly displays the top 20 names, scores, and stages. Do not use your real name or other personal information as your player name.

The web leaderboard is separate from Game Center and needs no account. Submitted records remain until we remove them on request or discontinue the service. To request deletion of your record, email us your player name, score, stage, and approximate submission time so we can identify it. We may be unable to delete a record if we cannot verify it belongs to you.

4. Ads, tracking, and purchases

The app and website have no ads, advertising SDKs, or analytics scripts added by us, and we do not track users across apps or websites. The app has no in-app purchases. No account is needed to play.

5. System permissions

The iPhone app does not request camera, photos, contacts, location, or microphone access.

6. Website services

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The web leaderboard uses Cloudflare Workers and D1. These providers may process connection and security logs under their policies. See the GitHub Privacy Statement and Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

7. Contact and updates

For privacy questions or leaderboard deletion requests, email mkjohnny@gmail.com. We will update the date and this page if our data practices change.
