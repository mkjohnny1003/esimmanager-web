Turn paper business cards into searchable, editable contacts.

Card Master recognizes Traditional Chinese and English text on your iPhone. No account is required. Always compare recognized details with the original card, especially names, phone numbers, and email addresses.

On-device recognition

Apple text recognition runs on your iPhone. Business card content is never sent to an external AI service.

Private cards

Lock selected cards and open them with Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode.

Encrypted backup

Export an encrypted backup and keep its recovery code separately for device changes.

One-time ad removal

All card features remain available in the ad-supported version. A one-time in-app purchase removes ads permanently. The App Store shows the local price before purchase.
