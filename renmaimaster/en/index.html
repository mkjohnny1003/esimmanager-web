FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common Questions

Clear answers about privacy, device migration, and ownership.

Without cloud servers, how do I transfer my cards when I buy a new iPhone?
Card Master includes an offline Portable Encrypted Backup feature. In Settings, you can export a secure AES-GCM encrypted backup file alongside a unique 256-bit Recovery Code. When you switch to a new iPhone, simply install Card Master, enter your Recovery Code, and all your cards (including photos and private cards) are restored in seconds—zero cloud dependency.
Can my employer, IT administrator, or CRM team see my saved cards?
Never. Card Master runs strictly inside your personal iPhone's iOS sandbox. There are no corporate admin portals, no team-sharing sync backends, and no surveillance mechanisms. Whether you change roles or companies, your contact roster remains your private, personal asset.
Does text recognition work offline or in airplane mode?
Yes, 100%. Card Master leverages Apple's native Vision framework and Neural Engine on your device. It requires zero network connectivity. You can take photos, scan cards, edit, and search seamlessly even in crowded convention centers or during flights.
Is the $0.99 ad-removal truly a one-time purchase?
Yes. We utilize standard App Store Non-Consumable In-App Purchases. Pay once ($0.99), and ads are gone permanently. You can restore your purchase on any new iPhone tied to your Apple account at any time without paying monthly or annual subscription fees.
Why don't you use OpenAI, ChatGPT, or cloud AI for scanning?
Sending business cards to external AI APIs exposes sensitive client phone numbers, private emails, and executive notes to third-party databases and training pipelines. We choose Apple on-device silicon specifically to ensure zero data leakage.
